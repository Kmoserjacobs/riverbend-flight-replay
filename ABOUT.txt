Riverbend flight replay — October 6, 2026

The viewer streams B1 and Power directly from the existing Luma captures.
The flight positions and gimbal angles were recovered from the supplied DJI recordings.
Use Explore, Follow the drone, or Drone camera; drag the timeline to inspect a position.
The view modes use icon buttons in the splat header, with tooltips and a visible
selected state. Colors, line width, and display options share this compact header.
Drone camera uses the
recording's 16:9 proportions on desktop and mobile.
Download track KMZ exports the selected area's recorded route for Google Earth,
with its one-second telemetry CSV inside the archive. Download mission KMZ exports
the separately reviewed Mavic 4 Pro mission validated in MavenRoute. These are
different exports: visual track display and repeat-flight mission programming.
The recorded video plays in sync with the flight position. Either set of playback
controls can play, pause or seek both; speed changes apply to both. Compact 720p
H.264 playback copies are included, derived from the DJI recording proxies.
Flight line and the compact display buttons activate or deactivate each feature.
Route and flown-line colors and pixel thickness are adjustable. A small 3D drone
and camera pyramid show the aircraft and recorded viewing direction in Explore
and Follow. The drone model uses an enlarged display size for readability.
Drone camera also shows an overhead route inset while Flight line is active.
Play and pause use symbols with accessible labels.

Fullscreen views
Use the fullscreen icon on the splat or recorded video. The opposite view stays
interactive in a lower-right inset. Its fullscreen icon swaps the main view.
Explore, Follow drone, Drone camera and Flight line remain available above the
fullscreen or inset splat. The same model, renderer and video remain loaded, so
playback time, camera position, alignment and display settings are preserved.
Exit with the main view fullscreen icon or Escape. If native fullscreen is
unavailable, the viewer expands within the page instead.

Ground projection and view highlighting are optional and off by default.
The full PLY exports are processed locally to create a roughly 200 KB combined
height surface; the browser continues streaming the existing hosted Luma models
and does not download another full splat. Ground rays use this approximate
surface. Corners beyond reconstructed ground are left open. The subtle adjustable
highlight colors the splat within the recorded camera view; it shows camera
coverage, not optical focus or survey accuracy. Display settings persist locally
and are included in copied replay links.

The GPS tracks are recorded telemetry. The placement of each splat is an estimate
from video landmarks, and should be checked against site dimensions or survey control.
Power placement is fitted from image camera centers across 72 views of the flight.
Its replay camera uses frame-level recorded angles plus image-derived heading,
tilt, roll and timing calibration. The GPS route and mission files are unchanged.
The fit was checked at intermediate times against the source video. The viewer
provides alignment adjustments and an export.
Relative altitude is measured against each recording's takeoff datum.
Recording start/end are not takeoff/landing. The viewer is a visualization, not a flight mission.
