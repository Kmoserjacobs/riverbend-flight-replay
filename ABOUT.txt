Riverbend flight replay — October 6, 2026

The viewer streams B1 and Power directly from the existing Luma captures.
The flight positions and gimbal angles were recovered from the supplied DJI recordings.
Use Explore, Follow the drone, or Drone camera; drag the timeline to inspect a position.

The GPS tracks are recorded telemetry. The placement of each splat is an estimate
from video landmarks, and should be checked against site dimensions or survey control.
Power has a provisional single-view placement, so scale and vertical position
remain uncertain. The viewer provides alignment adjustments and an export.
Relative altitude is measured against each recording's takeoff datum.
Recording start/end are not takeoff/landing. The viewer is a visualization, not a flight mission.

Both hosted model URLs must remain accessible for this viewer to load.
The webpage includes small reference frames; it does not include the source video
files or the large PLY exports.
